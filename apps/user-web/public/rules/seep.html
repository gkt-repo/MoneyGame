SEEP · seep-single-deal-v1

Build. Capture. Sweep. ♠

Choose a 2-player head-to-head table or a 4-player partnership table. Opposite seats (1 & 3, 2 & 4) are teammates. A table starts when all seats fill, after a 5-second countdown. This table plays one complete deal, rather than a multi-deal baazi.

The opening

The first seat receives four cards and bids 9–13 matching a card in hand. A hand without any valid bid is reshuffled. Four floor cards stay hidden until the bid. The first move must build a house at the bid value, capture with that value, or throw that value if it cannot capture. The rest of the first hand is then dealt: each player has 12 cards including the opening card already played.

With two players, another 12 cards each are dealt after the first hands run out; the floor stays in place. Four players use the whole deck in one hand. Play proceeds counterclockwise.

Your turn

Play one card. Ace = 1, Jack = 11, Queen = 12, King = 13. Choose a card, preview a legal move, then confirm. The highlighted floor items show exactly what the move uses.

Capture: A card captures a loose card of equal value, disjoint groups of loose cards adding to its value, and a house of equal value. Collect all available matching sets; when sums overlap, choose between the legal alternatives. A house cannot be combined with loose cards to capture a higher value.

Build: Combine your card with floor cards to make a house of 9–13, keeping another card matching the house value in hand. Owners must keep a matching card until their house is captured or raised. Matching houses and additional matching loose sets merge automatically.

Raise: Add one hand card to someone else’s open house to raise its value, while retaining a card of the new value. You cannot raise your own house, a cemented house, or use loose floor cards to raise a house.

Cement: Add a matching card or set to a house to make it pukka (unbreakable). Its value can no longer change. Opponents who cement become co-owners and must retain a matching card. You may add to your partner’s house using their reservation.

Throw: If the card cannot capture, you may leave it loose on the floor instead of building. You cannot throw a card that can capture or spend a house’s last reserved matching card elsewhere.

Points and sweeps

Every spade scores its capture value (1–13). Other aces score 1 each. The ten of diamonds scores 6. All other cards score 0. The deck contains 100 card points.

Clearing the floor scores a 50-point sweep, except the opening play (25 points) and the final play (0 points). At the end, the last side to capture receives any loose leftovers without a sweep bonus.

Result and entry

The side with the higher total of card points and sweep bonuses wins this single deal. All participants commit the displayed entry fee. The winners split the pool after the configured platform commission; any odd paise go to winners in seat order. A draw returns every entry without commission. There are no additional in-game bets.

Each turn lasts 30 seconds. On timeout, the server makes a legal move, prioritising a capture. Reconnecting restores your hand and the current table. If the server restarts, persisted Seep tables resume with a fresh turn timer. Cancellation uses the platform’s existing full-refund flow.

Rules references: Octro Seep and Pagat. The single-deal result and entry settlement above are this platform’s table format.
